# Online formats: webinar, podcast, AMA or LinkedIn Live

This file covers planning, choosing and running the format. The session content (webinar run of show with slides and talking points, podcast episode questions, AMA run of show, backup questions, guest briefings) belongs to event-speaker-kit; hand off to it. Every figure is a field ("[User to add: ...]").

## A. Webinar

**When to choose it:** discovery, nurture, and a fully relevant audience at low cost. In the method, webinars carry more of the plan when leads need nurture late in the year (choice-method.md section 7) and when there is no paid media budget. Attendance is a behaviour signal the follow-up engine can score.

**Without paid media:** run a series (webinar, LinkedIn Live or podcast) with similar partners and speakers; invite the people who reacted to past sessions; estimate registrations from the user's own past sessions, never from a benchmark [User to add].

**Running the day (outline; the detail is event-speaker-kit's):** the host opens with the session's thesis, introduces the panelists, runs timed questions, protects the audience questions (if the session runs long, drop a planned segment rather than the questions), and closes. Afterwards: the session video and a post event post.

**Registration analysis checklist (after each webinar, vertical neutral):**
- Registered, attended and not attended, with the conversion between them.
- Registrants by job category and by company type.
- Business email against personal email.
- Companies with more than one registrant.
- What is working; what needs improvement.
- Recommendations for the next webinar: drop down fields for job title and company type, a company size field for scoring, a business email requirement, an incentive to bring a colleague, referral partners, and attendance and post webinar engagement tracking.

**What to measure (fields):** registrations, attendance, share from target accounts, meetings, accepted leads, cost per accepted lead (by script).

**Common mistakes:** a hook statistic with no source; no registration fields to score fit; the questions squeezed out by overrun; no follow-up plan before the session.

**Hand offs:** the webinar run of show to event-speaker-kit; follow-up and no-show journeys to event-follow-up-engine.

## B. Podcast (as host or as guest)

**When to choose it:** authority with a niche audience over time, guest relationships with target accounts, and a low budget series. In the method's experience, distribution is the weak link: plan it before recording.

**Own show (template):** a positioning blurb for the audience; the show name (brainstormed separately); the recurring frame around each episode (the episode arc, its questions and their timings come from event-speaker-kit): introduction, sponsor message if any, theme music, topics with segues, an outro that recaps what listeners learned, a call to action, and contact details for promoting the episode after the show. Each themed segment can end with a follow-up question that gives the listener an actionable resource. Episode and segment lengths are [User to add].

**As a guest:**
- When to choose a show: by list match, that is how many of its listeners are the user's target buyers (choice-method.md section 1), using only what the host states about the audience [User to add]; never estimated.
- Preparation: the user writes their own talking points under the host's segments (introduction, core concepts, the user's framework, what has not been covered yet, a proudest moment, the key takeaway); the talking points themselves are speaker notes for event-speaker-kit. Confirm with the host how the user's name and company will be said on air.
- After: share the episode through the user's own channels and ask the host for the listener figures they are willing to share.
- What to measure (fields): conversations started with target accounts after the episode [User to add]; the host's figures only if the host shares them, labelled as the host's.

**Distribution plan:** where each episode goes, who shares it, and how guests are asked to share it [User to add: channels and owners].

**What to measure for an own show (fields):** listens or views on the user's own analytics, guests from target accounts, conversations started with guests, episodes shared by guests [User to add].

**Common mistakes:** recording before a distribution plan; guests chosen for fame rather than fit with target accounts; the firm or guest name said wrongly on air (confirm it before recording).

**Hand offs:** episode questions, the guest briefing and the run of show to event-speaker-kit.

## C. AMA or LinkedIn Live (online panel, often for a programme launch)

**When to choose it:** launching a programme, a report or a cohort to an online audience, with faculty or experts answering questions live.

**Plan (template):**
- Core theme set first, in one or two sentences the moderator holds through the session.
- Logistics: date and time with time zone, format, the promotion article, registration, and the programme the session leads to.
- Panel and the lens each panelist brings.
- A mapping from the published article to the session's topics, with topics parked for later sessions.
- A pre-live audience prompt (one word in the comments). No "comment a keyword to get the link" mechanic.
- Faculty discussion first, then audience questions; segment lengths are [User to add].

**Questions from the user's own people:** core members may each ask one question, in their own words, after the faculty discussion. The skill never writes questions for people to post as if they were strangers, never suggests fake or secondary accounts, and never plans coordinated comments made to look independent. The host may comment from their own profile at a spacing the user sets [User to add], never pasting all comments at once.

**What to measure (fields):** live viewers, registrations, questions from outside the core group, sign-ups to the programme [User to add].

**Common mistakes:** no core theme, so the session drifts; keyword for link mechanics; questions that look coordinated.

**Hand offs:** the AMA run of show, backup questions, scope lines and reserve closing line to event-speaker-kit; a programme launch release to pr-launch-pack.
