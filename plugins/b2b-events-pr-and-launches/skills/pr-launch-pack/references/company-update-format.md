# Company update post

For news shared from a company page or a leader's profile: funding, a partnership, a product milestone, team growth, an office move, a change of direction, an award.

## Inputs
- The news in one sentence, the audience (customers, partners, the industry, future hires), what changes for them, and why now.
- Every figure (amount, investors, partner names, headcount, award name and giver) from the user or a cited page, through claim-verification.md. Named investors, partners and customers need their approval.

## Structure (prose, writing rules apply)
1. Announcement: what is happening, stated plainly in the first sentence. No stock opener ("Thrilled to announce", "Humbled to share", "Excited to share", "Big news").
2. Background: why this happened, from the user's material.
3. What it means for customers or the industry: the change they will see, concretely, from the user's words or an opened page; otherwise "[User to add: ...]".
4. Details: timeline, scope, where it applies.
5. What is next: the next real step, only if it is decided.
6. Thanks: name the people or teams the user wants thanked, by role or name as the user gives them; no invented gratitude lines.
7. Call to action: one, and the link goes in the first comment on LinkedIn, not in the post body.

## Rules
- Focus on what changes for others, not internal celebration.
- No figures the user has not confirmed; no "leading" or "first" without a source.
- Two to three hashtags on LinkedIn, mixing one broad with niche ones. Tag only the people or companies the post is about.
- Count the post by script and keep it within the platform's limit.
- For funding news: only the amount, round and investors the user confirms are public; never state a valuation unless the user gives it with a source.
